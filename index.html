Choma Master Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Choma Master ("the game") is a mobile game made by j3rryl ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. Your game progress (levels, scores, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, app version)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. For data held by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 or the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
